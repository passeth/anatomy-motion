# Medical accuracy

These films teach patients. Every claim on screen or in the voice must trace to a source; when it cannot, cut it.

## Before animating

1. Write the cause chain in plain sentences and mark each with its source (textbook, guideline, review). Prefer society guidelines and reviews over single studies.
2. Numbers only from a source, with the source's own hedging: a modelled value gets "약" and a small source note (e.g. `Hansraj(2014) 계산 모델 추정치`).
3. Describe mechanisms at the level the evidence supports. Where a mechanism is debated (e.g. trigger-point physiology), use soft verbs ("~할 수 있어요", "부족해져요") rather than certainty.
4. No diagnosis, no cure or guarantee language, no "이것만 하면 낫는다", no product or treatment endorsements, no before/after promises.
5. End with when to see a professional, naming warning signs where they matter (numbness or weakness in the arm; jaw locking or limited opening; trouble swallowing, weight loss, vomiting blood or black stools with reflux).

## Disclaimers

- On screen, last 2 s: `…가 계속되면 ○○ 진료를 받아보세요` (one line, 32 px note).
- In the post caption: 교육용 정보이며 진단이나 치료를 대신하지 않습니다.

## If a clinic publishes it

Medical advertising is regulated in most countries (in Korea, 의료법 제56조 and the medical-advertising review system). Avoid testimonials, comparisons with other clinics, guarantees of results and exaggerated claims, and check whether the channel and reach require prior review before posting.
